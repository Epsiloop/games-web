Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026 • Last updated: November 4, 2026

This page explains how Epsiloop Games ("we", "our", or "us") handles data. Every game is a separate project, so each one has its own privacy details, listed per game below. The general rules in sections 1 to 4 apply to all of them and to the website at games.epsiloop.io.

1. The catalog website

This website sets no cookies and runs no advertising or analytics scripts. It loads no personal data from you beyond what any web request involves: your IP address and browser details are visible to our hosting provider and to Google Fonts (which serves the site's fonts). Your search and filter choices stay in your browser.

2. What we promise for all games

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • Each game lists what it collects below, and links to its full policy once it is released.
  • If a game starts collecting something new, we update its entry and the date before the change takes effect.
  • Purchases and ads, where a game has them, are handled by Google Play, the App Store or the named ad provider under their own policies.

3. Children

Our games are not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

4. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. Email info@epsiloop.io and name the game concerned. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Select your game below. Where a game has its own full policy, we link to it.

6. Changes to this page

We will publish updates at this address and change the date above. Continuing to play after a change takes effect means you accept the revised policy.
