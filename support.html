Support

Help for Epsiloop party games • Last updated: November 7, 2026

Need help with Party Games (Crocodile, Who Am I?, Mafia and Spy) or any other Epsiloop game? Start with the answers below. If they don't solve it, email us.

Contact us

Epsiloop Games — Support
Email: info@epsiloop.io

Please include the game name, your device model, the OS version and what happened. A screenshot helps. We usually reply within a few business days.

How to play

  • Crocodile: pass the device, one player sees the word and acts it out without speaking; the others guess.
  • Who Am I?: each player gets a hidden identity on their forehead or screen and guesses it with yes/no questions.
  • Mafia: everyone secretly sees their role in turn, then the group discusses and votes out suspects.
  • Spy: everyone sees the location except the spy, who must figure it out before being found.

Each game also has a rules card on its setup screen.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an internet connection?

No. All games and word lists are built into the app. Internet is only used to load ads.

Can I play on a TV?

Yes. The app works with a TV remote's D-pad as well as touch and keyboard.

The next player saw the word / role by mistake.

Hand the device over only after the screen is hidden. Start a new round from the game's setup screen to get fresh words or roles.

Words repeat or are missing in my language.

Words are shuffled without repeats until the list is used up. Tell us the language and category you want expanded.

The app crashes or looks wrong.

Update to the latest version, restart the app and, if that fails, restart your device. If it persists, email us the details above.

Ads or purchases.

Ads and any purchases are handled by Google Play, the App Store or the ad provider. For refunds, use the store's own refund process.

Delete my data.

The games store settings on your device only. Uninstalling the app removes them. For other requests see our Privacy page.
